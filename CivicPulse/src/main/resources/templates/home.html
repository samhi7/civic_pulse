About CivicPulse

CivicPulse is an advanced, automated citizen-municipal engagement platform designed to bring transparency and efficiency to local governance. By streamlining the feedback pipeline, we bridge the gap between citizens, field officers, and administrators.

Whether it is road maintenance, broken streetlights, or waste management anomalies, CivicPulse maps, logs, allocates resources, and tracks resolution timelines in real-time.
