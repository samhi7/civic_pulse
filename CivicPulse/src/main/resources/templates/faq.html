Help Center & FAQ

Find answers to common questions about filing complaints, assigning departments, and tracking resolutions.

CivicPulse is a digital transparency platform connecting local citizens with city administration. It enables users to report neighborhood issues such as broken streetlights or potholed roads, ensuring municipal departments can efficiently dispatch teams to resolve them.

Once logged in as a citizen, click the **+ Report New Issue** button on your dashboard. Choose an appropriate category (e.g. *Water Leakage*), write a summary title, specify the exact location/address, add details in the description box, and click **Submit Report**.

* **Pending**: The complaint has been registered and is awaiting review by the municipal administrator. * **In Progress**: The administrator has assigned the issue to a specific city department, and maintenance crews are active. * **Resolved**: The work is complete, and the issue is resolved.

Citizens can see issues reported by other users by clicking on the **Community Board** tab on their dashboard. Under the action column, click the **👍 Support** button next to any complaint. This increases its support count, signaling high priority to city planners.

The **Municipal Administrator** logs into the specialized administrator console (`admin@civicpulse.gov`). They review all public reports, select a responsible board (e.g. *Electricity Board* for lighting issues), type progress notes, and change the status. Standard citizens immediately see these updates when they log in.
